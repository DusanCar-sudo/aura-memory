Dusan's main motto (2026-09-16): "we build to solve anybody's problem." Making something that solves a real human problem is, to him, the highest truth.

**Why:** It's the guiding principle behind the [[github-warehouse]] ("raw ideas into finished product") and his projects.

**How to apply:** When shaping or scoping work, keep asking whose problem this solves and whether it actually solves it. Favor finished, usable fixes over half-built or impressive-looking ones.
